Studio philosophy

Less about decoration. More about how a space makes you feel.

We begin with the morning light and the walk from the front door to the kettle. Rooms are shaped for the ordinary hours first, and only then for the photograph.

Materials are chosen to age in public: plaster that takes a fingerprint, timber that darkens, stone that wears a soft path.

Space
Proportion and sequence before furniture.
Material
Few, honest, and left visibly worked.
Light
Drawn like a wall, planned from day one.
Daily life
Every detail tested against use.

Approach & services

One studio, from first sketch to the last object placed.

  1. 01

    Interior architecture

    Rebuilding rooms from the inside: structure, openings, joinery and services resolved together.

  2. 02

    Residential spaces

    Homes designed around how the household actually lives, not how a showroom is staged.

  3. 03

    Hospitality & commercial

    Restaurants, hotels and workplaces with a strong atmosphere that still runs well.

  4. 04

    Spatial planning

    Layouts and circulation studied in model and plan before a single wall is moved.

  5. 05

    Material & furniture curation

    Custom pieces and sourced objects, selected to belong to the room and to each other.

  6. 06

    Design consultation

    A focused day or two of advice for projects that need direction, not a full commission.
